CRICKET: A SHORT GUIDE
Document Version: 1.0
Owner: Learning Notes

1. WHAT IS CRICKET
Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players each. One team bats and tries to score runs, while the other team bowls and fields and tries to get the batters out. The game is played on a large grass field with a rectangular strip called the pitch in the middle.

2. FORMATS OF THE GAME
Cricket is played in three main international formats. A Test match lasts up to five days with two innings per side. A One Day International (ODI) has 50 overs per side and finishes in a single day. A T20 match has 20 overs per side and takes about three hours, which makes it the fastest and most popular format today.

3. BASIC RULES
Each over consists of six legal deliveries bowled by one bowler. A batter can be dismissed in several ways, including bowled, caught, leg before wicket (LBW), run out, and stumped. When the ball is hit to the boundary rope along the ground it scores 4 runs, and when it crosses the rope without bouncing it scores 6 runs.

4. PLAYER ROLES
A batter focuses on scoring runs. A bowler focuses on taking wickets and may be a fast bowler or a spinner. An all-rounder contributes with both bat and ball. The wicketkeeper stands behind the stumps, catches the ball, and can dismiss batters by stumping or catching. The captain makes tactical decisions such as bowling changes and field placements.

5. FAMOUS TOURNAMENTS
The ICC Cricket World Cup is held every four years for ODI cricket. The ICC T20 World Cup is the premier tournament for the shortest format. In India, the Indian Premier League (IPL) is a domestic T20 league that began in 2008 and attracts players and fans from around the world.

6. FAMOUS PLAYERS
Sachin Tendulkar is known as the highest run scorer in international cricket history. Virat Kohli is admired for his consistency and chasing ability in limited-overs cricket. Jasprit Bumrah is a leading fast bowler known for his unusual action and accurate yorkers. MS Dhoni captained India to victory in the 2007 T20 World Cup and the 2011 ODI World Cup.

7. FUN FACTS
The oldest international cricket match was played between the USA and Canada in 1844. The sport is followed by over a billion fans, with the largest audience in South Asia. A cricket ball is made of cork and wrapped in leather, and it weighs about 160 grams.
